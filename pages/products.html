Quick guide

Which product do you need?

Shipping dry, free-flowing cargo?

Grain, powders, pellets or granules in a standard container → Dry Bulk Container Liners

Shipping bulk liquid?

Non-hazardous liquid in volume, by container or trailer → Flexitanks for Liquids

Need pharma or chemical containment?

Controlled handling of powders or liquids in sensitive sectors → Containment Bags

Need a bespoke cover or liner?

Any shape, any size, standard or custom → Industrial Packaging

Need flexible on-site storage?

Water, fuel, effluent or fertiliser storage on site → Bladder Tanks & Agriculture
