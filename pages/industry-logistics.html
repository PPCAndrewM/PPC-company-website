The challenge

Bulk capacity without a bulk fleet.

Operators need to say yes to bulk cargo without buying, leasing or repositioning specialist equipment. Liners and flexitanks turn standard 20′, 40′ and 45′ containers — and trailers or reefer units — into bulk capacity on a per-shipment basis, then leave with the cargo. No empty-asset return freight.

  • Any standard container becomes bulk capacity — no specialist assets required
  • Faster turnaround — no tank cleaning between cargoes
  • 24/7 emergency response — inspection and cross-pumping if a unit is compromised in transit
