Overview

Six flexitank formats, one certified standard

A PPC Philton flexitank turns a standard ISO container, trailer or reefer unit into a single-use bulk liquid tank. Every format below shares the same manufacturing standard: food-grade co-extruded LLDPE film, high-tensile woven polypropylene reinforcement, and welds pressure-tested to 1 bar before the unit ever leaves our facility.

Multi-Layered

General-purpose bulk liquids, 10,000–24,000L, multiple loading configurations.

Single-Layered

Optimised for viscous products, semi-transparent for visual product level checks.

Trailer & Reefer

5,000–27,000L, multi-chamber anti-surge design for road transport.

Special Design

Bespoke engineering for hard-to-discharge or suspension products.

Static Storage

50,000L+ temporary storage for water and food-grade or industrial liquids.

Heater Pads

Re-liquify solidified oils, butters and greases during transit.

Applications

Where flexitanks replace drums, IBCs and dedicated tankers

Chemical & industrial liquids

Non-hazardous industrial chemicals, glycerine and similar products — always confirm compatibility with our technical team first.

Food & beverage ingredients

Wine concentrate, edible oils and other food-grade liquids using ISO 22000-certified materials.

Latex & natural rubber

Referenced directly in our MUTO partnership: over 5,000 units supplied for bulk latex transport since 2018.

Road, rail & reefer transport

Trailer and reefer-specific formats with anti-surge chambers for multi-modal transport.

Benefits

Why shippers move from drums and IBCs to flexitanks

Higher payload per container

A single flexitank typically carries more usable litres per container than an equivalent load of drums, with no empty-drum return freight.

Single-use, no cleaning cost

No tank-cleaning between cargoes and no cross-contamination risk between shipments.

Pressure-tested before dispatch

100% of end welds are pressure-tested to 1 bar — not sample-tested — before a unit leaves our facility.

Faster load and discharge

Top or bottom valve configurations are matched to your terminal equipment for faster turnaround than drum decanting.
