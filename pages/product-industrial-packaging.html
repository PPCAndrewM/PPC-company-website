Overview

Two ranges: shaped & special, or covers, liners & sheeting

Past projects include polythene covers for double-decker buses, pharmaceutical drum liners, food-grade containers and bulk trailer liners for contaminated-material transport — produced in our ISO 9001/14001/22000-certified facilities.

Shaped / Special Covers, Bags & Liners

Any shape or size, standard or complex designs, 58–1000 micron thickness, angled and shaped welds, natural or opaque finishes.

Covers / Liners, Films & Sheeting

Square/gusseted liners for boxes, films from 18–1000 microns, shrink-wrap pallet covers and custom specialty bags, from 500mm upwards.

Applications

Real projects, not just a catalogue

Vehicle & transport covers

Bespoke covers for vehicle cabs and bodies during production and transit.

Marine cargo covers

Weatherproof covers for cargo exposed to marine transport conditions.

Drum & IBC liners

Circular-based liners for 210 litre drums and square IBC liners.

Trailer & truck liners

Bulk trailer liners to stop contaminants leaking during transport.
