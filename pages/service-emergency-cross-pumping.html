Emergency Cross-pumping

Our Emergency Response Team facilitates safe transfers of Flexitank contents to alternative tank containers — protecting the product and keeping your shipment moving.

Talk to a technical contact When we cross-pump

How it works

Product out, safely, before anything is lost

During the cross-pumping process, we can also carry out additional actions such as removing foam or contaminants — and where a Flexitank has failed, we carry out a full inspection and root-cause analysis alongside the transfer to identify the source of the problem.

  • UK-based, with rapid nationwide response
  • Europe-wide operations for logistics companies & global Flexitank manufacturers
  • Handles concentrates, viscous goods, food-grade & non-food liquids

When we cross-pump

Common scenarios

Leaking Flexitank

Rapid response to damaged or leaking Flexitanks, transferring product into a new unit before it's lost.

Damaged container

When a shipping container is compromised in transit, we transfer product into a new tank container for safe continued handling.

Fermented or damaged product

Where product quality has been affected, transferring to a new Flexitank can help preserve what's salvageable.
