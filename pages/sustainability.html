Sustainability

Our approach to sustainability

We'd rather state plainly what we do today than claim more than we can back up. Here's where our environmental management currently stands, and the areas we're actively developing.

Where we stand today

Certified environmental management, recyclable material options

ISO 14001 certified

Independently audited environmental management system, awarded December 2019 and re-audited annually.

Recyclable materials, by default

Standard film and fabric options across our dry bulk liner, containment and industrial packaging ranges are fully recyclable. Foil laminate options, used specifically for moisture/thermal barrier performance, are the current exception.

Manufactured to last the journey

Pressure-tested, correctly-specified packaging reduces in-transit failure — and the waste, cleanup and replacement-shipment emissions that come with it.

What we're working on

Being direct about where we are

We haven't yet published quantified metrics such as recycled material content percentages or carbon-per-shipment figures, and we won't publish numbers here until we can stand behind them with the same rigour as our ISO certifications. If sustainability reporting is a requirement for your procurement process, contact our technical team directly — we're happy to discuss what we can currently evidence and where our roadmap is heading.
