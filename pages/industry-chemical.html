The challenge

Chemical cargo fails in three ways: reaction, contamination, containment

Chemical shippers carry a different risk profile from food or agricultural bulk cargo — a packaging failure isn’t just a commercial loss, it can be a safety and environmental incident. Our technical team works through the product's chemical compatibility, viscosity and any regulatory handling requirement before recommending a format.

  • Material compatibility — film and fabric options checked against the specific chemical before a format is recommended
  • Contamination control — single-use flexitanks and liners remove cross-batch contamination risk entirely
  • Containment assurance — every weld pressure-tested to 1 bar before dispatch, with a 24/7 emergency response service if something does go wrong in transit
