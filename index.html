How we work

From specification to loaded container

01

Specify the cargo

Tell us the commodity, volume and container type. Our technical team matches it to the right liner, flexitank or bespoke design — including chemical or food-grade requirements.

02

Engineer & sample

For bespoke requirements, we engineer a design and can provide samples for approval before committing to a production run.

03

Manufacture & test

Produced in our own ISO-certified facilities, with every flexitank weld pressure-tested to 1 bar and raw materials tested in-house.

04

Ship & support

Delivered through your chosen freight route, backed by installation training and a 24/7 emergency response team once in service.
